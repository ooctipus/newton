## Experiment discipline

- Write exact required physical work, avoidable intermediates, added work,
  fallback and synchronization costs before implementing a candidate.
- Preserve efficient existing parallel producers unless their replacement is
  explicitly part of the hypothesis. Tree sparsity, seven-arm/finger blocks,
  zero-impulse tangent skips and stationary-sweep exits already exist.
- Use a 90-minute first integrated checkpoint. A mismatch permits one targeted
  correction for an identified cause. No silent extension beyond two hours;
  record new evidence and a revised test before reopening.
- Compare complete current-state chains early, then actual live16K physics
  and synchronized wall time with cold/reset/contact transitions. Saved-state
  component timing is neither live physics nor training throughput.
- Do not sum overlapping stage durations, extrapolate row counts into elapsed
  savings, or claim a memory/compute ceiling without hardware-counter evidence.
- Record measured integrated gain, diagnosed loss or unvalidated hypothesis
  at every checkpoint. The 24-hour deadline is not automatic goal completion.
