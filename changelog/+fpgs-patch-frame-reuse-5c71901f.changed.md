Skip the contact pair sort of `SolverFeatherPGS` persistent patch friction (`friction_anchor_beta > 0`) on CUDA-graph-captured steps that reuse a contact frame, without a conditional graph node: a step captured in the same graph right after another captured step over the same `Contacts`, with no collision pass, `Contacts.clear()` or coupled-solver contact filter in between, keeps that step's sorted pairs. This holds on every substep of a collision pass after the first. Code that rewrites contact arrays between two such captured steps must do so through those writers: a step that finds its contacts rewritten otherwise (another `contact_generation`, count or `Contacts`) is solved without patch friction rows, releases its anchors and sets the new `patch_frames` flag of `constraint_capacity_status()`, on which `check_constraint_capacity()` raises. Set `FEATHER_PGS_PATCH_FRAME_REUSE=0` before construction to sort every step as before, small contact frames over their whole capacity; `get_path_selection()["routes"]["friction"]["frame_reuse"]` records the decision.
