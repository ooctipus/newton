Skip the contact pair sort of `SolverFeatherPGS` persistent patch friction (`friction_anchor_beta > 0`) on CUDA-graph-captured steps that reuse a contact frame, without a conditional graph node: a step captured in the same graph right after another captured step over the same contact stream (the same `contact_generation` array, which every object wrapping the arrays of one `Contacts` shares), with no write of that stream by Newton in between (a collision pass, `Contacts.clear()`, a coupled-solver contact filter or `SolverMuJoCo.update_contacts()`), keeps that step's sorted pairs. This holds on every substep of a collision pass after the first. It narrows the capture contract for code that rewrites contact arrays between two such captured steps itself, including passing other contact arrays with the same `contact_generation` array: call `Contacts.clear()` before the rewrite, which makes the next step sort, or set `FEATHER_PGS_PATCH_FRAME_REUSE=0`. Otherwise only a change of `contact_generation` or of the contact count is detected: that step is solved without patch friction rows, releases its anchors and sets the new `patch_frames` flag of `constraint_capacity_status()`, on which `check_constraint_capacity()` raises. A rewrite that keeps both but changes which shapes a contact slot pairs, or reorders the contacts, is not detected, and that step groups its contacts by the previous step's sorted pairs; rewriting only contact points, normals or margins is exact. Set `FEATHER_PGS_PATCH_FRAME_REUSE=0` before construction to sort every step as before, small contact frames over their whole capacity; `get_path_selection()["routes"]["friction"]["frame_reuse"]` records the decision.
