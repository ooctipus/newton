FeatherPGS chooses the native sparse factor and the compact diagonal of the sparse-diagonal response only where the held tree takes their groups; elsewhere ordinary dynamics take dense factors. ``cached_dynamics='off'``, ``FEATHER_PGS_TREE_KINETIC_STATE=0`` and ``FEATHER_PGS_MIXED_TREE_KINETIC_STATE=0`` now keep the dense representations, as do articulations whose root joint is revolute, prismatic or a D6 joint with coordinates (held groups take fixed or free roots); give such an articulation a fixed base body to keep a sparse representation. ``get_path_selection()`` no longer reports ``routes.sparse_mass_matrix.factor_kernel``.
