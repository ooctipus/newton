Add the experimental `mf_gs_parallel_linked_velocity_limit_pass` option to `SolverFeatherPGS`: the parallel tiers that serve persistent patch friction end with an ordered, push-only velocity-limit pass, so a tier stopped by its sweep budget still leaves the joint velocity limits the last word, as the serial sweeps do. It changes nothing once a tier converges, applies only with `enable_joint_velocity_limits`, and `get_path_selection()` reports it under `routes.sweep.tiers.velocity_limit_pass`.
