Write persistent patch friction's (`friction_anchor_beta > 0`) contact rows with the local row packets of `SolverFeatherPGS` wherever the packets already wrote point friction's (on CUDA, worlds of one limited articulation and at most one other, such as Franka lift) instead of with ordinary rows; a Franka lift task steps about 7% faster. The rows agree with the ordinary ones to rounding; a bouncing normal row sums its incident velocity per body, as the point packets do, so a contact whose incident velocity lies within a rounding of a bounce threshold can bounce on one route and not the other. Set `FEATHER_PGS_ROW_PACKETS=0` before construction to keep these worlds on ordinary rows, the previous behavior; the variable switches the packets off for point friction too, and `get_path_selection()["routes"]["contact_rows"]["row_packets"]` records the decision.
