Added `CollisionPipeline(mesh_sdf_candidate_filter=True)`: the two-stage mesh-SDF path prunes candidates that cannot yield a reduced contact. The cull drops, while sweeping a context's edges, every candidate that cannot be an inner contact and whose Lipschitz lower bound (midpoint SDF minus edge radius) already exceeds the best midpoint registered for its reducer voxel (so the work segments are written compacted, deterministically), and the solve kernel keeps only the candidates within each segment whose lower bound does not exceed the segment's best midpoint of the same voxel, plus the six axis extremes of the possibly-inner candidates. On dense assembly scenes this removes about half of the edge searches and reducer inserts while keeping the deepest contact per voxel and normal bin; exported contacts are a subset of the unfiltered ones. Off by default.
