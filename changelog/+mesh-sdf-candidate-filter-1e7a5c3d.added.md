Added `CollisionPipeline(mesh_sdf_candidate_filter=True)`: the two-stage mesh-SDF path runs the edge search only on the candidates of each work segment that can still yield a reduced contact (Lipschitz lower bound against the segment's best midpoint SDF of the same reducer voxel, plus the six axis extremes of the possibly-inner candidates per voxel). On dense assembly scenes this halves the edge searches and reducer inserts while keeping the deepest contact per voxel and normal bin; the exported contacts are a subset of the unfiltered ones. Off by default.
