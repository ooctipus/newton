Drop candidate pairs whose shapes are both structurally immobile in the `CollisionPipeline` broad phase by default, in every broad phase mode. Such shapes are static or belong to a dynamic body welded to the world, whose every parent joint is an enabled fixed joint from the world or from another welded body; kinematic bodies count as movable. Contacts between two structurally immobile bodies are no longer generated, as PhysX generates no static or kinematic-static contacts: contact sensors no longer report them, and solvers that resolve fixed joints as constraints no longer respond to them (`SolverFeatherPGS` already gave them no response). Pass `include_immobile_pairs=True` or set `NEWTON_BROAD_PHASE_IMMOBILE_FILTER=0` to generate them again.
