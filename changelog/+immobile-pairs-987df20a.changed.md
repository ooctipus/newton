Drop candidate pairs whose shapes are both structurally immobile in the `CollisionPipeline` broad phase by default, in every broad phase mode. Such shapes are static or belong to a dynamic body welded to the world, whose every parent joint is an enabled joint without degrees of freedom, such as a fixed joint or a D6 joint without axes, from the world or from another welded body; kinematic bodies count as movable. Contacts between two structurally immobile bodies are no longer generated, as PhysX generates no static or kinematic-static contacts: contact sensors no longer report them, and solvers that resolve such joints as constraints no longer respond to them (`SolverFeatherPGS` already gave them no response, but the remaining contacts may be renumbered, and on CUDA a new row order changes its results as any change of contact order does). With `include_static_kinematic_pairs=False`, a body welded to the world also counts as static, so its pairs with kinematic shapes are dropped as well. Pass `include_immobile_pairs=True` or set `NEWTON_BROAD_PHASE_IMMOBILE_FILTER=0` to generate them again. Code that changes `Model.joint_enabled` or `Model.body_flags` at runtime must also call `CollisionPipeline.notify_model_changed()` with `ModelFlags.JOINT_PROPERTIES` or `ModelFlags.BODY_PROPERTIES`; otherwise a body released from its welding joint keeps no contacts with static shapes. `SolverCoupledADMM` keeps these pairs in its own contact pipeline.
