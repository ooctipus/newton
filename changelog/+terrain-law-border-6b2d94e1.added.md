Give the terrain law the heightfield's border: the law takes a heightfield as a solid block with vertical side walls from its border down 1 m, as the terrain route's prisms are. A body hanging over the border gets the top face's contacts at its deepest points over the footprint, clipped at the border's edge, and a body beside the border gets contacts on the walls, the border's edges and vertices and the vertical lines at the footprint's corners.
