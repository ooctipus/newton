Add the closed-form cylinder row to the typed heightfield cell queries: a flat cap yields four rim points, a rolling cylinder its side line, a tilted one its lowest rim point, each clipped to the cell footprint and resolved against the prism by the shared point query. It replaces the GJK cylinder split owner by default; `NEWTON_HEIGHTFIELD_ANALYTIC_PRIMITIVES=0` restores the generic queries and the split.
