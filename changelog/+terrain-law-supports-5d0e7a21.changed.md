Keep every support of a terrain pair in the experimental `NEWTON_TERRAIN_LAW_SHADOW` side buffer: a pair's rows are no longer capped at 8, 16, 16 or 24 per partner class, nor its candidates at 32, so rough terrain with many supports in reach (a 0.25 m sphere hovering over it, a hull with 30 supports) no longer raises `terrain_rows`. A pair with more rows than its walk slots writes all of them to a spill region whose rows the new experimental `NEWTON_TERRAIN_LAW_SHADOW_ROWS` environment variable sets (default: from the listed pairs' own bounds on their rows, the largest pair's and 1/256 of their sum). `terrain_rows` now means that this region overflowed, in which case the pairs that found no room keep their most important rows (each plane group's deepest first) up to their walk slots and the warning prints the rows to set, or that a shape's scale, margin or gap grew after the collision pipeline was built (rebuild the pipeline).
