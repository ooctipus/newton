Keep every support of a terrain pair in the experimental `NEWTON_TERRAIN_LAW_SHADOW` side buffer: a pair's rows are no longer capped at 8, 16, 16 or 24 per partner class, nor its candidates at 32, so rough terrain with many supports in reach (a 0.25 m sphere hovering over it, a hull with 30 supports) no longer raises `terrain_rows`. Rows beyond a pair's walk slots go to a spill region whose rows the new experimental `NEWTON_TERRAIN_LAW_SHADOW_ROWS` environment variable sets (default: as many as the walk slots of every listed pair, and at least the most candidates one pair can give); `terrain_rows` now means that this region overflowed (raise the variable to the rows the printed warning asks for) or that a shape's scale, margin or gap grew after the collision pipeline was built (rebuild the pipeline).
