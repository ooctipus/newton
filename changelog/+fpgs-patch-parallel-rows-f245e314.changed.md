Solve persistent patch friction (`friction_anchor_beta > 0`) in the parallel matrix-free tiers and keyed rows of `SolverFeatherPGS` when `mf_gs_parallel_rows` requests them, as point friction already does: in the tiers' Jacobi sweeps each tangent pair projects on its patch ring's pooled normal load with one isotropic step, the serial pair solve's friction law, and each tier sweeps `mf_gs_parallel_linked_sweeps` times (`"auto"` by default), so contact-rich patch worlds keep the serial sweep's accuracy on a faster route. The tiers accumulate joint velocity-limit rows; worlds they decline (more dense rows than the tier budget, or free-body rows) keep the serial sweep and its `velocity_limit_law`, stateless by default. Solvers that request no parallel rows are unchanged. Set `FEATHER_PGS_PATCH_PARALLEL_ROWS=0` to keep patch friction on its serial sweeps and ordinary rows; `get_path_selection()["routes"]["friction"]["parallel_rows"]` records the decision.
