Solve persistent patch friction (`friction_anchor_beta > 0`) in the parallel matrix-free tiers and keyed rows of `SolverFeatherPGS` when `mf_gs_parallel_rows` requests them, as point friction already does. In the tiers' Jacobi sweeps each tangent pair projects on its patch ring's pooled normal load and steps isotropically with the smaller of its two tangent steps, the serial pair solve's friction law, so a sliding pair converges to the serial sweep's maximal-dissipation fixed point. The tiers accumulate joint velocity-limit rows (`routes.sweep.tiers.velocity_limit_law`), so they share the serial sweep's fixed point where it accumulates them too (`velocity_limit_law="accumulated"`) or without joint velocity limits, as `routes.friction.parallel_rows` reports; worlds they decline (more dense rows than `mf_gs_parallel_rows` allows, or free-body rows) keep the serial sweep and its `velocity_limit_law`, stateless by default. The linked tier kernels' names carry `_linked_iso`. Solvers that request no parallel rows are unchanged. Set `FEATHER_PGS_PATCH_PARALLEL_ROWS=0` to keep patch friction on its serial sweeps and ordinary rows; `get_path_selection()["routes"]["friction"]["parallel_rows"]` records the decision.
