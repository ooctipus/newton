Keep `CollisionPipeline(rigid_contact_max=...)`, `CollisionPipeline.rigid_contact_max`, `Model.rigid_contact_max` and `ModelBuilder.num_rigid_contacts_per_world` for compatibility with Newton main; none of them sizes the contact buffers, whose first mapping takes the shape-pair estimate even when they are set. They resolve as on Newton main, the argument, else a positive `Model.rigid_contact_max`, else an estimate from the model's shape pairs, written back to `Model.rigid_contact_max`, by which `SolverKamino` still sizes its fixed contact container.
