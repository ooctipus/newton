`GlobalContactReducer(dense_pairs=...)` adds a dense pair table: shape pairs that carry a dense index address their reduction slots directly (`GlobalContactReducerData.dense_values`, one entry per pair and bin) instead of probing the hashtable, `export_reduced_contacts_kernel` scans the table's used prefix after the hashtable's active entries, and `clear_active(dense_pair_count=...)` zeroes that prefix contiguously. The two-stage mesh-SDF path uses the table for its first 65,536 mesh-mesh pairs (fast reduction mode without hydroelastic data; later pairs keep the hashtable) through `export_and_reduce_contact_centered_two_spatial_depths_dense` and `export_and_reduce_predictive_contact_dense`; reduced contacts are unchanged. Direct launchers of `export_reduced_contacts_kernel` pass four more inputs (`dense_values`, `dense_stride`, `dense_bins`, `dense_pair_count`).
