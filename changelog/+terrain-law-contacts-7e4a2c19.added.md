Add the experimental `NEWTON_TERRAIN_LAW` environment variable, which lists terrain-law partner classes (`point`, `box`, `cylinder`, `hull` or `all`) whose heightfield pairs the law writes instead of the terrain route: the narrow phase drops those pairs, and after it the law's rows become contacts through the stock contact writer, one reservation per pair, with the heightfield as shape A, the row's normal and points, and the row's window-local feature id as sort key. A hull-class partner without a core in the law (a barrel cylinder) keeps the terrain route. It cannot be combined with `NEWTON_TERRAIN_LAW_SHADOW` or speculative contacts.
