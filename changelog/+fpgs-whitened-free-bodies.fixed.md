FeatherPGS free-body contact rows now use the mass matrix the dynamics factor, joint armature included: each row is whitened by its body's held factor (``Z = L^-1 J^T``) and the sweeps run on the bodies' factor-coordinate velocities. The rows store 12 numbers per row instead of 24 (``mf_Z_a``/``mf_Z_b`` replace ``mf_J_a``/``mf_J_b`` and ``mf_MiJt_a``/``mf_MiJt_b``; ``mf_body_Hinv`` is removed) and the colored sweep's slots half as many.
