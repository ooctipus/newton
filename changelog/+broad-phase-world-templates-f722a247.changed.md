Test model-built explicit contact pairs per world template in `CollisionPipeline` when replicated worlds share large pair templates (at least 2**20 pairs and 64 pairs per world): each world stages its shapes' bounds once instead of streaming the flat `Model.shape_contact_pairs` list, and the candidate pairs are the same set. `get_collision_selection()` reports the chosen kernel under `narrow_phase.broad_phase_kernel`. Set `NEWTON_BROAD_PHASE_TEMPLATES=0` before constructing the pipeline to restore the flat kernel, or `1` to test templates of any size.
