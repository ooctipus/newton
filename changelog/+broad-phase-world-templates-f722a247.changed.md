Use per-world pair templates in the explicit broad phase of `CollisionPipeline` when `Model.shape_contact_pairs` is the list `ModelBuilder.finalize()` replayed from them and the templates are large (at least 2**20 template pairs and a mean of 128 pairs per world): each world stages its shapes' bounds once instead of streaming the flat list, and the candidate pairs are the same set. `get_collision_selection()` reports the chosen kernel and the reason under `narrow_phase.broad_phase_kernel`. Set `NEWTON_BROAD_PHASE_TEMPLATES=0` before constructing the pipeline to keep the flat kernel, or `1` to use templates of any size.
