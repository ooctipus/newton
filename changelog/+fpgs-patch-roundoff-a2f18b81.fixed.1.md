Keep each FeatherPGS patch support edge and its friction rows on the same contacts between steps. An edge takes every contact within 0.1% of the footprint's extent of it, and its last contact in contact order carries its rows, instead of whichever tied corner float32 roundoff placed farthest along the footprint's axis. Resting rectangular boxes near or far from the world origin are no longer kicked at each change, and sliding boxes yaw far less at low iteration counts. Footprints within a few percent of square more than about 50 m from the origin can still change edges, and a collision pipeline that reorders a pair's contacts between steps can still move the rows, though not the anchor locations.
