Converted the Newton contacts of the in-step MuJoCo Warp path into world-contiguous contact id ranges (`nconmax` slots per world) with per-contact refresh records when MuJoCo Warp offers `Callback.contact_records`: MuJoCo Warp's fused constraint stage then reads each world's contacts from its range, refreshes their `dist`/`pos` from the body poses of the substep and applies the row predicate itself, so `SolverMuJoCo` no longer runs a by-contact refresh pass and MuJoCo Warp no longer buckets the contact pool on every substep. `Data.nacon` still counts the contacts, but their ids no longer form the prefix `[0, nacon)`; consumers must map Newton rows to MuJoCo ids through the solver's row map, as the Isaac Lab force copy does. Older MuJoCo Warp versions keep the dense id pool.
