Fold the per-substep Newton to MuJoCo Warp adapter launches in `SolverMuJoCo`: controls, joint forces, body forces and the contact-conversion prelude run as one kernel; the pre-step wake pass replaces `sleep.update_sleep_trees` with a `tree_awake` refresh that also snapshots the converted contact count and detects wake events, and the collision wake reports the trees it wakes (a tree woken by contact in the first substep after a collide now receives its dormant contacts in that substep); the joint-state re-sync detects edited trees while converting instead of copying through a scratch buffer; `opt.timestep` is written once per tick. Sleep and contact bookkeeping arrays are unchanged; floating-point results differ only within solver round-off. No migration needed.
