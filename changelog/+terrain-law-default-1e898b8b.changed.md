Make the terrain law the default source of heightfield contacts in `CollisionPipeline`: the heightfield pairs of spheres, capsules, boxes, cylinders, cones, ellipsoids and convex hulls get one contact per support the law finds in the pair's window of the grid (a terrain face, edge or vertex within reach), written after the narrow phase, instead of the terrain route's reduced per-triangle contacts. Supports on edges and creases are kept and no pair that penetrates the terrain over its footprint is left without a contact, which the terrain route did on rough terrain; some scenes get more contact rows. Configurations the law does not support yet keep the terrain route: speculative contacts, the `NEWTON_TERRAIN_LAW_SHADOW` comparison mode and a prebuilt narrow phase with its own contact writer. `get_collision_selection()["heightfield"]["law"]` records whether the law writes and why. Set `NEWTON_TERRAIN_LAW=none` before constructing the pipeline to keep the terrain route, a temporary opt-out until that route is removed; a class list or `all` still requests the law and raises on an unsupported configuration. `NEWTON_TERRAIN_LAW_SHADOW_ROWS` and `NEWTON_TERRAIN_LAW_SHADOW_JOBS` now tune the law whenever it runs. The law builds its terrain features from the heights at construction: rebuild the collision pipeline after editing `Model.heightfield_elevations` in place.
