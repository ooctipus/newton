Speed up the mesh-SDF narrow phase by testing collision edges in spatial clusters of 16 against per-cell lower bounds of the texture SDF before culling them one by one. The cull is exact: contacts are unchanged. `Model` gains `mesh_edge_order`, `mesh_edge_clusters` and `mesh_edge_cluster_reach`, and `NarrowPhase.launch()` takes them with the other precomputed edge arrays; callers that pass `mesh_edge_centers` and `mesh_edge_halves` must pass them too to keep the precomputed-edge path.
