Fix `SolverFeatherPGS` producing non-finite states for long articulated chains without joint armature: along a serial chain the float32 factorization's pivots fall roughly as the cube of the chain's length, and a 100-link chain's mass matrix met a negative pivot within a few steps. Where an articulation with a root-to-leaf path of at least 32 joints has a pivot below `4 n u` of its diagonal at the model's configuration, the solver now adds the least joint armature that lifts it there, warns, and reports it under `routes.dynamics.armature_floor`; models without such pivots keep their armature unchanged. Give the joints armature to choose it yourself.
