Fix `SolverFeatherPGS` producing non-finite states for long articulated chains without joint armature: along a serial chain the float32 factorization's pivots fall roughly as the cube of the chain's length, and a 100-link chain's mass matrix met a negative pivot within a few steps. Each tree is now factored by a leaf-first walk in local frames, whose float32 pivots stay positive without added armature to at least 1,000 links; the solver adds no armature of its own.
