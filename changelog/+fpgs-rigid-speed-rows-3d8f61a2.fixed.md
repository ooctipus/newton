Fix `SolverFeatherPGS` bounding a free rigid body's velocity components instead of its speed: `rigid_body_max_linear_velocity` and `rigid_body_max_angular_velocity` (PhysX's `maxLinearVelocity` and `maxAngularVelocity`) now bound the magnitudes of the body's linear and angular velocity, so a body moving along a diagonal no longer reaches up to `sqrt(3)` times its limit. Each finite limit is one speed row (two per body instead of twelve per-axis rows) that the sweeps project onto the limit along the current velocity, under either `velocity_limit_law`; `velocity_limit_activation_fraction` gates it on the speed.
