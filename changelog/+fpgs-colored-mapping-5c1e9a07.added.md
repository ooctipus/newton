Sweep the free-body contact rows of `SolverFeatherPGS` worlds that hold many contacts on many bodies with the colored mapping: a block per world visits the world's contacts in color order, the contacts of a color at once, each color being the contacts' level in row order, so the sweep stays the row-order Gauss-Seidel sweep. At every pack each world takes it when its contact units and their units per color pass the measured crossover of the two mappings (32 units and 1.75 units per color); more such worlds than the device has streaming multiprocessors run in waves, each of which raises the units per color a world needs, and worlds whose free bodies cannot reach that many units per color hold no storage for it. `get_path_selection()["routes"]["sweep"]["mf_colored"]` records the kernel, the rule, its waves, the capable worlds and their storage.
