Remove the experimental register-state copy of the 64-row `tiled_row` sweep of `SolverFeatherPGS`. It no longer matched the default sweep's friction projection: it projected each tangent row on its own, left out the other normal rows of a friction patch from the friction bound and skipped friction rows with a nonpositive diagonal. `FEATHER_PGS_DENSE_ROW_REGISTERS` selects nothing and is recorded as unknown; unset it. `FEATHER_PGS_DENSE_ROW_BUDGETS=1` still selects the 64- and 128-row storage budgets, and `get_path_selection()["routes"]["sweep"]["dense"]` no longer has a `register_state` entry.
