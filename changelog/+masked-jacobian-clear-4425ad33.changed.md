Zero only the selected articulations' blocks of a supplied Jacobian, instead of the whole padded Jacobian, in `newton.eval_jacobian` calls with a `mask` and in `newton.selection.ArticulationView.eval_jacobian` calls with a `mask` or on a view that does not cover every articulation, on CUDA devices when the blocks are large and in graph captures of a call into a non-contiguous `J`. Other such calls, including every eager call on the CPU, still zero the whole Jacobian. Values in selected blocks, padding included, are the same as before. Blocks of articulations outside the view or the mask are now unspecified: either zeroed or left holding the buffer's previous values. Call `J.zero_()` first if code reads them. With `J=None` they are still zero.
