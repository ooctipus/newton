Solve the fused contact triplets of `SolverFeatherPGS`'s native sparse sweep (the experimental `FEATHER_PGS_SPARSE_CONTACT_TRIPLETS=1` mode) as contact units on their Gram blocks under either friction law. The sparse contact builder writes a block record per triplet (the normal's inverse diagonal, the couplings and the pair's eigenbasis), and the sweep loads it with the triplet's rows, reduces its three residuals together, moves the pair's residuals by the normal's change through the couplings and applies the three changes in one pass; rings of up to four normals pool their loads from a member list. In exact arithmetic this is the row-by-row sweep, so results change by rounding only. The staged triplet transaction and its metric cache go, with `FEATHER_PGS_SPARSE_TRANSACTION`.
