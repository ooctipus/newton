Fuse the contact triplets of persistent patch friction (`friction_anchor_beta > 0`) in a single-stage transaction under the experimental `FEATHER_PGS_SPARSE_CONTACT_TRIPLETS=1` mode of `SolverFeatherPGS`'s native sparse sweep. The sparse contact builder writes a block record per triplet, and the sweep loads it with the triplet's rows and reduces its three residuals together, without the bounded metric cache or its capacity; rings of up to four normals pool their loads from a member list. The paired friction law is unchanged, so results change by rounding only. Set `FEATHER_PGS_SPARSE_TRANSACTION=staged` to keep the previous kernels, or `=single` to use the transaction for point friction too. The solver rejects other values at construction.
