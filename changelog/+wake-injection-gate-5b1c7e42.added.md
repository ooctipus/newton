Launch the dormant-contact injection of woken trees in `SolverMuJoCo` on every substep (the launches exit early without a wake event) instead of under a conditional graph node, whose scheduling cost in a CUDA graph (about 9 us per substep, taken or not) exceeded the launches it skipped. The new `SolverMuJoCo.conditional_wake_injection` attribute (default `False`) restores the conditional node. Results are unchanged either way.
