Add `SolverMuJoCo.conditional_wake_injection` (default `True`). Set it to `False` to launch the dormant-contact injection of woken trees on every substep instead of under a conditional graph node; the launches exit early without a wake event, and in large batches where some tree wakes on most substeps this removes the conditional node's scheduling cost (about 10 us per substep in a CUDA graph). Results are unchanged either way.
