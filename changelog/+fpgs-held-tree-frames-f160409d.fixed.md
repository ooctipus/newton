Accept joint-frame changes in the held-tree cached dynamics of `SolverFeatherPGS`: re-posing a fixed-base articulation's root through `Model.joint_X_p`, as Isaac Lab does on resets, now invalidates and repairs the caches instead of raising that the solver must be reconstructed. This holds for `ModelFlags.JOINT_PROPERTIES` and for any other notification that carries a flag besides `JOINT_DOF_PROPERTIES`, `BODY_INERTIAL_PROPERTIES`, `SHAPE_PROPERTIES` and `MODEL_PROPERTIES`, such as `ACTUATOR_PROPERTIES` alone. A notification whose joint frames and axes are unchanged keeps the caches, and a changed joint axis still raises. Cached mass factors take a moved frame at their next mass refresh, as ordinary dynamics' mass matrix does; `reset()` requests that refresh for the worlds it selects.
