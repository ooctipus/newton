Sweep the dense rows of `SolverFeatherPGS`'s matrix-free sweep only over the world coordinates their Jacobian or response touches (a bitmap of the coordinates, built once per launch from the rows), instead of over every coordinate of the world; each lane keeps its coordinates and their order and skips only exact zeros. A world of one articulation among many free bodies (the clutter scenes) no longer sweeps its articulation's rows over every free body's coordinates. The smaller kernels change how the compiler contracts the friction pair's Newton norm, so trajectories can differ at the rounding level.
