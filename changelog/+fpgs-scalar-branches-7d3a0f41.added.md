Add the default-off experimental `FEATHER_PGS_SCALAR_BRANCHES=1` scalar-branch family to the held-tree cached dynamics of `SolverFeatherPGS`. A diagonal-mass group whose articulations are world-fixed roots over one-coordinate prismatic or revolute leaves, such as a keyboard's keys, runs one thread per coordinate without the tree plan's 64-body cap, and the held-tree owner then also owns the other articulations of those worlds. Where admitted it supersedes `FEATHER_PGS_PRISMATIC_PUBLICATION` and `FEATHER_PGS_PRISMATIC_LINEAR_STATE`; `SolverFeatherPGS.get_path_selection()` reports it under `routes.held_tree.scalar_branches`.
