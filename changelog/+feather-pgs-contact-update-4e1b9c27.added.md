Add the experimental `contact_update` option to `SolverFeatherPGS`, with the `FEATHER_PGS_CONTACT_UPDATE` environment override. `"unit"` solves each contact unit exactly against its own Delassus block with every other impulse held: a point contact's normal and friction together (take-off, else stick, else Daviet's single-unknown slide root), a patch region's ring normals and anchor pairs as one unit, and a ring of more than 8 normals or 2 anchors as anchor units with the ring's other load held; a unit whose load change keeps reversing is relaxed toward its exact solution. Every solution of the contact problem is a fixed point of the `"unit"` sweeps: where a contact's own problem has several solutions (sliding friction that presses its normal down), the unit keeps the one it holds, and an anchor whose take-off fails where only friction that presses its normal carries load takes the press slide of the larger load. A slide the unit holds whose load answers the contact's approach with the wrong sign, or more than eight times its normal row's own response, takes its normal with that friction held, then the friction, which keeps it and stabilizes sweeps that the coupling of units would drive away from it. The contact law is unchanged; `"unit"` solves a contact's normal and friction together instead of stepping its normal row against stale friction, which cycles with period two for a sliding contact whose friction lifts its normal and can end a solve with no normal impulse on an approaching contact; the held slide's step never unloads its contact. The default `"sequential"` keeps the existing update and kernels. Only the loop sweeps implement `"unit"` (the dense `pgs_kernel="loop"` sweep of split mode and the CPU free-body sweep); every other sweep keeps `"sequential"` with a construction warning, and `get_path_selection()` reports the law each sweep runs under `routes.sweep.contact_update`.
