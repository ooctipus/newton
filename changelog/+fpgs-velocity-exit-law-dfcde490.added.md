Add the experimental keyword-only `pgs_velocity_change_tolerance` option to `SolverFeatherPGS` [m/s or rad/s]: the native Gauss-Seidel sweeps stop a world once no row's impulse change times its effective-mass diagonal (the change of the row's own velocity) exceeds it, and sweeps that visit only the rows selected at sweep entry also wait until no zero-impulse unilateral row is violated by more. Zero, the default, keeps the exact stationary-sweep exit and every generated kernel unchanged; `get_path_selection()["routes"]["sweep"]["exit_law"]` names the sweeps that honor a positive tolerance, and the solver warns when some do not. `pgs_solve_readout="trace"` adds, per solve, the sweep at which each tolerance from 1e-7 to 1e-1 would have stopped it, its exit reason, and its final sweep's largest velocity-scaled change by row kind.
