Sort the patch-friction contact pairs of `SolverFeatherPGS` under a conditional CUDA graph node: captured graphs skip the sort for empty contact frames and on steps that receive the contact frame sorted last (the same `Contacts`, `contact_generation` and contact count), which is every second substep of a collision pass in two-substep setups. Code that rewrites contact arrays between captured steps without a collision pass must advance `contact_generation` (for example with `Contacts.clear()`) or change the count. The node pauses the capture once per step, so an event recorded before `step()` cannot be waited on after it; a step taken while a stream forked by the caller still holds unjoined work sorts unconditionally. Set `FEATHER_PGS_PATCH_CONDITIONAL_NODES=0` to keep the sort unconditional; `get_path_selection()["routes"]["friction"]["conditional_nodes"]` records the choice and its reason.
