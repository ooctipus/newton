Deprecate `SolverFeatherPGS`'s `parallel_tree` option without replacement; stop passing it. Its cooperative tree kernels are removed: `parallel_tree=True` now warns, has no effect and runs the ordinary kinematics, inverse-dynamics and publication kernels, or the held-tree owner, which is tree-parallel on CUDA. `get_path_selection()["routes"]["dynamics"]` no longer has a `parallel_tree` entry.
