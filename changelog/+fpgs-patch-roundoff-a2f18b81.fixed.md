Keep FeatherPGS patch friction from swapping the anchor diagonal of a square or other isotropic contact footprint on float32 roundoff. Contact order and member spans now choose the anchor pair, and only footprints whose second moments differ by more than 0.1% take a principal axis, so resting boxes away from the world origin no longer receive a friction kick at each swap. A collision pipeline that reorders a pair's contacts between steps can still swap the pair.
