Add the experimental `velocity_limit_law` option to `SolverFeatherPGS`, with the `FEATHER_PGS_VELOCITY_LIMIT_LAW` environment override. `"accumulated"` solves the dedicated joint and free-body velocity-limit rows of every matrix-free sweep as unilateral rows with an accumulated impulse, so the converged velocity no longer depends on the row order and matches the parallel tiers; the default `"stateless"` keeps PhysX's clamp, and the `"physx_grasp"` schedule always keeps it. Split mode, which has no joint velocity-limit rows, applies neither: where its free-body sweep has velocity-limit rows, it keeps the stateless clamp that `routes.velocity_limits.free_body_sweep_law` reports. `get_path_selection()` reports the serial sweeps' law under `routes.velocity_limits.law`, and the parallel tiers' law, accumulated under either option, under `routes.sweep.tiers.velocity_limit_law`.
