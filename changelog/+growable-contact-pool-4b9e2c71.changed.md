Grow rigid contact buffers from measured demand instead of allocating a configured or estimated `rigid_contact_max` up front. The `Contacts` arrays, the collision pipelines' sort, matching and body-pair reduction buffers and the per-contact state of `SolverFeatherPGS` and `SolverVBD` share one set of rows per model: they start at 4,096 rows and grow together, keeping their contents, up to what the device memory can back; a growth it cannot back raises `MemoryError` before changing anything. A measured demand sizes them to 2.5 rows per contact: the state that `CollisionPipeline.service_capacity(state)` measures before a graph is captured, and a `CollisionPipeline.collide()` outside graph capture that fills the rows, which grows them before anything reads them. On CUDA with the optional `gpu_components` package the buffers are CUDA virtual memory: each field reserves addresses for every row the device could back and maps only the rows in use, so growth keeps their addresses; without it, as on the CPU, they are ordinary arrays of the ready rows that growth reallocates and copies. `Contacts.rigid_contact_max` is the rows the arrays hold now; read the arrays from `Contacts` after a growth rather than keeping references, and `Contacts(rigid_contact_max, ...)` built directly takes it as its initial rows. A graph replays fixed buffer extents: call `CollisionPipeline.service_capacity()` once per step outside capture and recapture when it returns `True`; it grows the buffers once the recorded contact high-water mark passes 60% of the rows, to 2.5 rows per contact of the mark, and warns when a replayed pass reached the rows (its capacity status stays flagged).
