Grow rigid contact buffers from measured demand instead of allocating a configured or estimated `rigid_contact_max` up front. The `Contacts` arrays, the collision pipelines' sort, matching and body-pair reduction buffers and the per-contact state of `SolverFeatherPGS` and `SolverVBD` share one set of rows per model, which grows together, keeping its contents, to 2.5 rows per measured contact, up to what the device memory can back; a growth it cannot back raises `MemoryError` before changing anything. On CUDA with the optional `gpu_components` package the buffers are CUDA virtual memory: each field reserves addresses for every row the device could back and maps pages only for the rows in use, starting with every row its first pages hold, so growth keeps their addresses and joins no stream; without it (or with a `gpu_components` release that predates the device graph updater's clamped and scaled extents, which logs a warning), as on the CPU, they are ordinary arrays that start at 4,096 rows and that growth reallocates and copies. A `CollisionPipeline.collide()` outside graph capture that fills the rows grows them before anything reads them, and a pass clamps `rigid_contact_count` to the rows it wrote, flagging the drop, so consumers read every row below the count (`SolverFeatherPGS.constraint_capacity_status()['contacts']` is kept and is always `False`). Captured graphs follow the growth: the pipeline's contact-sized launches, its sort, matching and reduction, and those of `SolverFeatherPGS` are captured at the device bound and bound to a device graph updater that runs each at the contact count of its replay. Call `CollisionPipeline.service_capacity()` once per step outside capture: it maps more rows, without synchronizing the device, once the recorded contact high-water mark passes 60% of the rows, and returns `True` only when a live graph must be recaptured (a consumer read `Contacts.rigid_contact_max` during its capture, or the rows were reallocated). The replay whose contacts first outnumber the rows, and the replays already launched when the service runs, drop the contacts past them and flag it. `Contacts.rigid_contact_max` is the rows the arrays hold now; read the arrays from `Contacts` after a growth rather than keeping references, and `Contacts(rigid_contact_max, ...)` built directly takes it as its initial rows.
