Grow rigid contact buffers from measured demand instead of allocating a configured or estimated `rigid_contact_max` up front. The `Contacts` arrays, the collision pipeline's sort, matching and body-pair reduction buffers and the per-contact state of `SolverFeatherPGS` and `SolverVBD` start at 64 rows per world (at least 4,096) and grow, keeping their contents, up to what the device memory can back; a growth it cannot back raises `MemoryError` before changing anything. On CUDA with the optional `gpu_components` package they are CUDA virtual memory: each field reserves addresses for every row the device could back and maps only the rows in use, so growth keeps their addresses; without it, as on the CPU, they are ordinary arrays of the ready rows that growth reallocates and copies. `Contacts.rigid_contact_max` is the rows the arrays hold now; read the arrays from `Contacts` after a growth rather than keeping references, and `Contacts(rigid_contact_max, ...)` built directly takes it as its initial rows. A `CollisionPipeline.collide()` outside graph capture grows the buffers before anything reads them. A graph replays fixed buffer extents: call `CollisionPipeline.service_capacity()` once per step outside capture and recapture when it returns `True`; it grows the buffers to four times the recorded contact high-water mark once it passes half the ready rows (a replay's demand may double before it drops a contact, and the buffers grow again only once it doubles), and warns when a replayed pass reached the ready rows (its capacity status stays flagged).
