Plan each stage of a `SolverFeatherPGS` step once at construction: the factor, the predictor's solve, the contact response and the Delassus assembly of every articulation size, and the solve's driver, are one table each that `step()` launches through and that `get_path_selection()` records under `routes.stages`, so the record and the launches cannot disagree. Construction builds only the kernels these tables launch: the triangular-solve, response, fused-response and Delassus kernels of the routes not taken, the split-mode free-body sweep in matrix-free mode and the metadata pack without free rigid bodies are no longer built (`routes.sweep.mf_meta_pack.kernel` is then `None`), and the grouped response solve is built at construction instead of at its first launch. `routes.dynamics.grouped` reports each size's route and reason from the same table.
