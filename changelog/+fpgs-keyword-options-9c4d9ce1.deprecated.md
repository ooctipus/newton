Deprecate passing `SolverFeatherPGS` options positionally. Every argument after `model` is now keyword-only; positional calls of the options that were positional before (`angular_damping` through `friction_anchor_beta`) keep working during the deprecation period and emit a `DeprecationWarning` that names the keywords to use, while the options that were already keyword-only still raise `TypeError` when passed positionally. Pass options by keyword, for example `SolverFeatherPGS(model, angular_damping=0.1)` instead of `SolverFeatherPGS(model, 0.1)`.
