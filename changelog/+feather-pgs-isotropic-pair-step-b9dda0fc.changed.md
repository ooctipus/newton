Step each friction pair of the `SolverFeatherPGS` parallel tiers that serve persistent patch friction (opt-in with `FEATHER_PGS_PATCH_PARALLEL_ROWS=1`) with the smaller of its two tangent steps, so a sliding pair converges to the serial patch sweep's maximal-dissipation fixed point instead of a friction direction turned towards its stiffer tangent. The tiers share the serial sweep's whole fixed point only where both accumulate joint velocity-limit rows (`velocity_limit_law="accumulated"`, or no joint velocity limits), which `routes.friction.parallel_rows` reports. The tier kernels' names gain `_iso`, and `get_path_selection()` reports `routes.sweep.tiers.pair_step`. Point-friction tiers keep per-row steps. No migration is required: the tiers stay opt-in with `FEATHER_PGS_PATCH_PARALLEL_ROWS=1`, and solvers without it run the same kernels as before.
