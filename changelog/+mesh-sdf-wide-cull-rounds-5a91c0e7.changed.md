Restructured the two-stage mesh-SDF narrow phase. The cull kernel tests four edges per lane per round with the texture fetches in flight together, appends candidates to a shared ring through warp-aggregated atomics and flushes full 256-candidate segments at the round end, instead of pushing every edge through a block-synchronous stack (about nine block barriers and 256 serialised shared atomics per 256 edges before). Segments now pack the candidates of consecutive pair contexts (each item records its context; `SDF_WORK_SEGMENT_STRIDE_INT32` grew from 2 + 2 x 256 to 2 + 3 x 256 ints), so the solve kernel's lanes no longer idle on per-context tails. With `mesh_sdf_candidate_filter=True` the streaming prune decides after a whole 1024-edge round has registered its midpoints, so the pruned set is independent of the edge order and dominated candidates within a round are dropped too, except possibly-inner candidates that are one of the six axis extremes of their voxel (a stronger, still deterministic, application of the same rules); the solve kernel's per-segment gate is gone because the round-scope prune subsumes it. Contexts whose candidates or hits do not fit the scratch buffer are still redone whole by the fallback.
