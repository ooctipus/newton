Add the opt-in `pgs_solve_readout` option to `SolverFeatherPGS`. With `"sweeps"`, the native Gauss-Seidel sweeps count, per kernel and world, how many sweeps each solve executes and whether it stops before its budget, the velocity pass of `pgs_velocity_iterations` apart from the position solve, without changing any computed value; solves of worlds without rows, which the matrix-free sweeps do not enter, count as skipped. Under `articulated_contact_response="propagation-fused"`, whose fused kernel runs every solve, nothing counts. `get_path_selection()` reports the cumulative counts under `measurements["solve_readout"]` (reading them synchronizes the device once) and names the counted kernels under `routes["sweep"]["readout"]`. With `"trace"` they also record, per solve, the sweep at which each velocity tolerance from 1e-7 to 1e-1 [m/s or rad/s] would have stopped it, its exit reason (no change, changes within the exit law's floor, or the budget) and its final sweep's largest change of a row's own velocity, by row kind: the residual the exit law reads, from the kernels the solver runs.
