Add the opt-in `pgs_solve_readout` option to `SolverFeatherPGS`. With `"sweeps"`, the native Gauss-Seidel sweeps count, per kernel and world, how many sweeps each solve executes and whether it stops before its budget, the velocity pass of `pgs_velocity_iterations` apart from the position solve, without changing any computed value; solves of worlds without rows, which the matrix-free sweeps do not enter, count as skipped. Worlds with rows that sweeps without counters own, such as those of the parallel tiers of `mf_gs_parallel_rows`, are counted by neither. Under `articulated_contact_response="propagation-fused"`, whose fused kernel runs every solve, nothing counts. `get_path_selection()` reports the cumulative counts under `measurements["solve_readout"]` (reading them synchronizes the device once) and names the counted kernels and the owners of the uncounted worlds under `routes["sweep"]["readout"]`.
