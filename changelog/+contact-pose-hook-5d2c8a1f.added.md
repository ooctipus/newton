Refresh the pose-dependent fields of converted Newton contacts inside the MuJoCo Warp step through its `Callback.post_position` hook (when the installed MuJoCo Warp provides it), so converted contacts no longer read Newton `body_q` between substeps. The new `SolverMuJoCo.publish_intermediate_body_state` attribute (default `True`) lets substepping integrations skip the intermediate forward kinematics for `body_q` / `body_qd` when nothing reads Newton body state before the finalizing `step` of a tick.
