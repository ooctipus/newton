Add the convex-mesh row to the closed-form heightfield cell queries: the hull vertices within admission distance of the cell plane form the touching polygon (up to eight directional extremes), which is clipped to the cell footprint like the box face, with the lowest vertex always reported by the cell under it; it runs inside the generic fallback scan rather than as another typed kernel. `NEWTON_HEIGHTFIELD_ANALYTIC_CONVEX=0` keeps hulls on the generic query. Clipped box and hull vertices take their plane depth directly instead of the general point query, which could answer with a side wall for points on the cell boundary, and each cell keeps at most the four deepest clipped points of a face.
