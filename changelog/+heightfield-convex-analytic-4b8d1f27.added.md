Add the convex-mesh row to the closed-form heightfield cell queries: the hull vertices within admission distance of the cell plane form the touching polygon (up to eight directional extremes), which is clipped to the cell footprint like the box face, with the lowest vertex always reported by the cell under it. The row runs inside the generic fallback scan and is opt-in (`NEWTON_HEIGHTFIELD_ANALYTIC_CONVEX=1`): on hulls resting near terrain it measured slower than the generic query and its per-pair cost is not yet located. Clipped box and hull vertices now take their plane depth directly instead of the general point query, which could answer with a side wall for points on the cell boundary.
