Add `Contacts.reserve_rigid_contacts()`, which grows a `CollisionPipeline.contacts()` buffer to the contacts a solver writes into it, and `Contacts.rigid_contact_bound`, the rows its arrays can grow to. `SolverMuJoCo.update_contacts()` and `SolverKamino.update_contacts()` use it instead of raising when such a buffer starts below `naconmax` (MuJoCo's collision) or Kamino's contact capacity; with Newton's contacts `SolverMuJoCo` needs only the rows it converted. During graph capture the growth waits, with a warning, for `CollisionPipeline.service_capacity()`; until then `rigid_contact_count` keeps the solver's count, the rows past `rigid_contact_max` are dropped, and `SolverMuJoCo.contact_capacity_status()` flags `source_contacts`.
