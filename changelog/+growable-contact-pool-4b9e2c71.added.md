Add `Contacts.reserve_rigid_contacts()`, with which a writer of its own declares the contacts it may write into a buffer, which grows to hold them, and a `state` argument to `CollisionPipeline.service_capacity()`, which measures that state's rigid contact demand and sizes the buffers for it, so a graph captured after it holds every contact from its first replay. `SolverMuJoCo.update_contacts()` and `SolverKamino.update_contacts()` declare MuJoCo's `naconmax` or Kamino's contact capacity instead of raising when the buffer holds fewer rows; with Newton's contacts `SolverMuJoCo` needs only the rows it converted. During graph capture a reservation waits, with a warning, for `CollisionPipeline.service_capacity()`; until then the rows past `rigid_contact_max` are dropped, `rigid_contact_count` holds the rows kept, and `SolverMuJoCo.contact_capacity_status()` flags `source_contacts`. `newton.examples.run()` sizes each example's buffers for its starting state before the first frame (recapturing its graph then only if the buffers could not hold that state, otherwise after the first frame), services its collision pipelines after every frame and recaptures its graph when the buffers grow.
