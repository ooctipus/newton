Add `Contacts.reserve_rigid_contacts()`, with which a writer of its own declares the contacts it may write into a buffer, which grows to hold them, and a `state` argument to `CollisionPipeline.service_capacity()`, which measures that state's rigid contact demand and grows the buffers if it fills them, so a graph captured after it holds every contact from its first replay. `SolverMuJoCo.update_contacts()` and `SolverKamino.update_contacts()` declare MuJoCo's `naconmax` or Kamino's contact capacity instead of raising when the buffer holds fewer rows; with Newton's contacts `SolverMuJoCo` needs only the rows it converted. During graph capture a reservation waits, with a warning, for `CollisionPipeline.service_capacity()`; until then `rigid_contact_count` keeps the writer's count, the rows past `rigid_contact_max` are dropped, and `SolverMuJoCo.contact_capacity_status()` flags `source_contacts`. `newton.examples.run()` makes each example's starting state fit before its first frame, services its collision pipelines after every frame and recaptures its graph when the buffers grow.
