Add the experimental `mf_gs_parallel_linked_sweeps` option to `SolverFeatherPGS`: the sweep budget of the parallel tiers that serve persistent patch friction, which point-friction tiers do not share (they keep `mf_gs_parallel_sweeps`). The default, `"auto"`, gives each linked tier `ceil(15/4 * pgs_iterations * sqrt(R / 32))` sweeps for its row bound `R`, at least `mf_gs_parallel_sweeps` and at most 128 unless `mf_gs_parallel_sweeps` is larger, so contact-rich patch worlds of every size reach the serial sweep's accuracy; every world still exits early at `mf_gs_parallel_tol`. A positive count sets every linked tier's budget, and `None` uses `mf_gs_parallel_sweeps`. The environment variable `FEATHER_PGS_PATCH_PARALLEL_SWEEPS` (a count or `auto`) overrides the argument, and `get_path_selection()` reports each tier's budget and its rule under `routes.sweep.tiers`.
