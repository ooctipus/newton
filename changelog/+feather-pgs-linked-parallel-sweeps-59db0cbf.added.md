Add the experimental `mf_gs_parallel_linked_sweeps` option to `SolverFeatherPGS`: the sweep budget of the parallel tiers that serve persistent patch friction, which otherwise share `mf_gs_parallel_sweeps` with the point-friction tiers. A positive count sets it; `"auto"` gives each linked tier `ceil(15/4 * pgs_iterations * sqrt(R / 32))` sweeps for its row bound `R`, at least `mf_gs_parallel_sweeps` and at most 128 unless `mf_gs_parallel_sweeps` is larger, and every world still exits early at `mf_gs_parallel_tol`. The environment variable `FEATHER_PGS_PATCH_PARALLEL_SWEEPS` overrides the argument, and `get_path_selection()` reports each tier's budget and its rule under `routes.sweep.tiers`.
